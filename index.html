Create your study plan

Enter your available time to calculate a manageable revision schedule.

This is a suggested schedule. Adjust it to suit your needs and remember to take breaks.

How to use your plan

1. List the topics you need to revise.

2. Start with the topics you find most difficult.

3. Practise questions and review your mistakes.

4. Leave time to review everything before your exam.

About this tool

This free calculator helps students estimate their available revision time and divide topics into manageable sessions. It is a planning aid, not a guarantee of exam results.

Privacy

This calculator processes your entries in your browser. It does not send them to a server or save them.
