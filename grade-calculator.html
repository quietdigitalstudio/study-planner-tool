Each bar shows an assessment’s percentage score. Its contribution also depends on its weight.

How the numbers work

Weighted contribution: (mark ÷ maximum mark) × weight. Your completed-work average is the total contribution divided by completed weight, multiplied by 100.

Target calculation: (target − points earned) ÷ remaining weight × 100. Remaining weight includes all weight not yet completed, even if you have not added a row for it. Weights above 100% are rejected.

Use your course’s actual assessment weights. This tool does not convert percentages into letter grades. It keeps one working calculation in this browser; clearing browser data can erase it. Use Print / Save as PDF in your browser if you want to keep a snapshot.
