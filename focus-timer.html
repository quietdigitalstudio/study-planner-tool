How to use your focus timer

  1. Choose a topic or task to work on.
  2. Start a 25-minute focus session.
  3. Keep distractions away and work on one task.
  4. When the timer ends, take a short break.
  5. Repeat when you feel ready.

These durations are a starting point. Adjust your study routine to suit your needs, and take breaks whenever you need them.
